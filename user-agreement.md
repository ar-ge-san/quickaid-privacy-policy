## What is Quick Aid?

Quick Aid is a safety application designed to provide peace of mind for individuals who live alone, have medical conditions, or want additional security. The app offers:

- **Scheduled Safety Check-ins**: Regular automated check-ins to ensure your wellbeing
- **Emergency Contact Alerts**: Automatic notifications to trusted contacts if you don't respond
- **Location Sharing**: Precise location sharing during emergency situations
- **SOS Functionality**: Quick access to emergency services and contact notifications
- **Multi-language Support**: Available in English, Turkish, German, and French

## How It Works

### 1. Setup Process
- Create your account with basic information
- Add trusted emergency contacts
- Configure check-in intervals (e.g., every 6 hours)
- Grant necessary permissions (location, notifications)

### 2. Daily Operation
- App sends gentle notifications asking "Are you OK?"
- Tap the confirmation button to indicate you're safe
- If you don't respond within the grace period, contacts are alerted
- Use SOS button for immediate emergency assistance

### 3. Emergency Response
- Missed check-ins trigger automatic alerts to contacts
- Your precise location is shared with emergency contacts
- Emergency services (112/911) can be contacted directly
- Follow Me mode allows live location sharing
