# Privacy Policy for Quick Aid

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025

## Introduction

Ar-ge-san ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Quick Aid (the "App").

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, phone number, country
- **Profile Data**: Profile photo, preferred language, theme preferences
- **Emergency Contacts**: Names and phone numbers of trusted contacts you designate
- **Device Information**: Device ID, push notification tokens, app version

### Location Data
- **Precise Location**: GPS coordinates when you enable location sharing
- **Location History**: Stored temporarily for emergency alert purposes
- **Background Location**: Used only when "Follow Me" mode is active

### Usage Data
- **Check-in Logs**: Timestamps of safety check-ins and responses
- **Emergency Events**: Records of SOS activations and missed check-ins
- **App Activity**: Feature usage patterns and settings preferences

### Media Data (Optional)
- **Photos/Videos**: Only if you choose to capture and share media
- **Audio**: Microphone access for emergency video recording

### Technical Data
- **Crash Reports**: Anonymous crash data to improve app stability
- **Analytics**: App usage statistics (anonymized)
- **Logs**: Technical logs for debugging and support

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Purposes
- **Safety Monitoring**: Conduct scheduled check-ins and emergency alerts
- **Emergency Response**: Share your location with trusted contacts during emergencies
- **Communication**: Send notifications and alerts to you and your contacts
- **App Functionality**: Provide core safety features and user experience

### Secondary Purposes
- **App Improvement**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
- **Support**: Provide customer service and technical support
- **Legal Compliance**: Comply with applicable laws and regulations

## Data Storage and Security

### Storage Locations
- **Primary**: Firebase (Google Cloud) - secure cloud storage
- **Local**: Encrypted data on your device for offline functionality
- **Retention**: Data retained as long as your account is active

### Security Measures
- **Encryption**: All data encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Access Controls**: Strict access limitations to personal data
- **Authentication**: Secure user authentication and session management
- **Regular Audits**: Periodic security assessments and updates

## Data Sharing

### With Trusted Contacts
- **Emergency Situations**: Location and status shared only during emergencies
- **Check-in Alerts**: Notifications sent when you miss scheduled check-ins
- **SOS Activation**: Immediate alerts with your location when SOS is triggered

### With Third Parties
- **Firebase/Google**: Cloud storage and push notification services
- **Emergency Services**: Direct calls to 112/911 when SOS is activated
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or legal process

### We Do NOT Share
- Your personal data with advertisers
- Your location data for marketing purposes
- Your contact information with third parties (except emergency contacts)

## Your Rights and Controls

### Data Access
- View all personal data we have collected
- Export your data in a portable format
- Request corrections to inaccurate information

### Data Control
- **Delete Account**: Request complete account deletion
- **Data Portability**: Export your data before deletion
- **Contact Management**: Add/remove emergency contacts anytime
- **Location Controls**: Enable/disable location sharing
- **Media Controls**: Choose whether to share photos/videos

### Communication Preferences
- **Notifications**: Control notification types and frequency
- **Emergency Alerts**: Cannot be disabled for safety reasons
- **Marketing**: No marketing communications sent

## Children's Privacy

Quick Aid is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Parents should supervise their children's use of the app to ensure appropriate usage of safety features. If you become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately.

## International Users

### Data Transfers
- Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own
- We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers
- Data processing complies with applicable privacy laws

### Regional Rights
- **EU Users**: Additional rights under GDPR
- **California Users**: Additional rights under CCPA
- **Other Jurisdictions**: Rights as provided by local law

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any material changes by:
- Posting the updated policy in the App
- Sending email notifications (if applicable)
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
